Clean up

Brush over a small unwanted object — a cable, a stray item, a bit of clutter — sitting on a plain background like a wall, floor, or ceiling. This is a basic local fill, not real AI object removal: it blends in nearby colors rather than reconstructing texture or pattern, so it works best on simple areas and will look smudgy on busy backgrounds or large objects. Treat it as a light touch-up, not a guarantee — check the result before saving.

Prefer not to brush? Auto-clean scans the photo itself and fills in small isolated spots — dust specks, tiny marks — on its own. It only touches small, isolated blemishes (never large areas), so it will miss anything bigger and occasionally miss a spot entirely. Use it as a first pass, then brush over anything it left behind.

Runs entirely client-side in your browser via <canvas>. No server, no upload, no storage.
